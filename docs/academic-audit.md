# Academic activity audit

This review maps the game's learning activities to the Common Core kindergarten standards and the U.S. Institute of Education Sciences foundational-reading practice guide. It distinguishes standards-aligned practice from enrichment and general play. The game is supplemental practice, not a comprehensive curriculum or assessment.

## Literacy

| Activity | Intended skill | Finding | Alignment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest Sounds | Identify medial vowels and final phonemes in familiar CVC words | Spoken directions, named picture choices, explicit model, two practice rounds, immediate feedback | Directly aligned to CCSS RF.K.2.d and IES foundational-reading guidance |
| Rhyme Garden | Recognize rhyming words | Spoken target and picture choices; model plus practice | Aligned to CCSS RF.K.2.a |
| Word Treats | Recognize common high-frequency words | Target is spoken and initially taught with say-spell-say; child matches speech to print | Aligned to CCSS RF.K.3.c and IES guidance for high-frequency words |
| Letter Skywriting | Print letters | Three letters use ordered start points and stroke paths, with spoken guidance | Aligned introductory practice for CCSS L.K.1.a; the letter set should continue expanding |
| Sound Bridge | Blend phonemes into a familiar word | Three spoken phonemes are blended and matched to a picture | Aligned to CCSS RF.K.2.b and IES explicit phonological-awareness guidance |
| Word-Cracker Eggs | Segment familiar words into phonemes | Spoken words are modeled as three sounds and represented by three hatching eggs | Aligned introductory practice for CCSS RF.K.2.d |

## Other developmental activities

- Heartbeat Check practices auditory attention, imitation, timing, and working memory. It is not currently a music-standard assessment because every four taps succeeds regardless of rhythm accuracy.
- Recovery Room Memory practices visual memory and matching. It is cognitive play, not a direct kindergarten academic standard.
- Enchanted Search and X-Ray Detective practice visual discrimination and persistence, not a direct academic standard.
- Coloring and tracing support stylus control and fine-motor practice. Coloring is creative play; current tracing should not be interpreted as handwriting mastery.
- Vet care, teeth cleaning, tic-tac-toe, dress-up, and story exploration are primarily imaginative play, sequencing, strategy, and engagement.

## Product conclusions

1. The strongest standards-aligned activities are sound identification, rhyming, high-frequency-word recognition, counting small requested quantities, and addition/subtraction stories within 10.
2. Double-digit recognition remains deliberately advanced enrichment, not kindergarten-level mastery. It stays optional and does not replace work with quantities and numerals 0–20.
3. Guided tracing now validates ordered points and start direction, though the available letter and numeral set should continue expanding.
4. Math now includes pictured quantities, direct comparison, a double ten-frame, shapes, measurement, and sorting.
5. The app provides broad supplemental coverage but is not a complete kindergarten curriculum or a formal assessment.
